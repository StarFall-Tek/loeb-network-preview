Our process

  1. Listen. A confidential first conversation.
  2. Gather. Collect documents and public records.
  3. Verify. Separate what is proven from what is alleged.
  4. Present. Build a sourced, structured case file.
  5. Act. Advocate through the appropriate channels.
What we are not. The Loeb Network is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Where legal counsel is needed, we will encourage you to consult a licensed attorney.
