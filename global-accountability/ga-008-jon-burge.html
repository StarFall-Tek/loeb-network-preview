Global Accountability GA-008

Jon Burge: A Police Commander Convicted of Lying About Torture

United States (Chicago) · a sourced Global Accountability entry built from court records and official findings.

Every statement below carries a source ID that links to the Sources table.

Entry info

CountryUnited States (Chicago)
ThemePolice cover-up; perjury and obstruction
OutcomeConvicted June 2010 of two counts of obstruction and one of perjury; sentenced to 54 months on Jan 21, 2011 [S-01]
StatusConviction (perjury and obstruction only) [S-01][S-02]

1 Summary

Former Chicago Police Department Commander Jon Burge was convicted in June 2010 of two counts of obstruction of justice and one count of perjury [S-01]. The charges were based on false sworn answers he gave in a 2003 civil case, in which he denied using, or knowing of other officers using, improper coercion, physical abuse or torture on suspects in custody at Area Two [S-01][S-02]. On January 21, 2011 he was sentenced to 54 months in prison followed by three years of supervised release, an upward departure from the guidelines sentence [S-01][S-03]. The sentencing judge said she did not believe his denials [S-03]. Burge was never criminally charged with the abuse itself [S-03]. A special prosecutor's investigation concluded that the abuse fell outside the statute of limitations [S-01][S-03].

2 Follow the money

The cited sources document no bribes, payments, fines, forfeitures or settlement figures for this case [S-01][S-02][S-03].
