Global Accountability

Documented cases of child exploitation and abuse of official power around the world: convictions, court judgments and official inquiry findings, each one sourced.

Inclusion standard: only matters with an adjudicated outcome (conviction, guilty plea, court judgment) or an official inquiry finding. Each entry has a summary, a “Follow the money” section, a dated timeline and a sources table, and every statement carries a source tag. People named are presumed innocent of anything not adjudicated.

Ten entries chosen by Christopher Loeb from the October 4, 2026 shortlist. Each entry is limited to what a court decided or an official inquiry found. Every factual sentence carries a numbered source tag (S-01, S-02 and so on) that points to that entry's sources table. Each "Follow the money" section lists only amounts stated in the cited sources. Where the sources document no money trail, the section says so.

How entries are built

Proven, not alleged

Summaries state only what a court decided or an official inquiry found. Acquittals and collapsed prosecutions are reported as such.

Follow the money

Bribes, payments, fines, forfeitures and settlements, using only figures stated in the cited sources. If no money trail is documented, the entry says so.

Source for every line

Every statement links to a numbered source: government reports and court records first, then major news organisations.
