Global Accountability GA-002

IICSA: A Nation's Institutions on Trial

United Kingdom (England & Wales) · a sourced Global Accountability entry built from court records and official findings.

1 Summary

The Independent Inquiry into Child Sexual Abuse published its final statutory report on October 20, 2022, and it was presented to Parliament under section 26 of the Inquiries Act 2005 [S-01][S-02]. The report sets out the Inquiry's findings on how far state and non-state institutions failed in their duty of care to protect children from sexual abuse and exploitation [S-02][S-03]. It draws on 15 investigations and 19 investigation reports and makes 20 recommendations, which add to the 87 recommendations in earlier investigation reports [S-03]. The report cites estimates that 1 in 6 girls and 1 in 20 boys experience child sexual abuse before the age of 16 [S-03]. Its three central recommendations are mandatory reporting, a national redress scheme, and a Child Protection Authority in England and in Wales [S-03].

2 Follow the money

The report recommends a fixed-term national redress scheme for England and for Wales "to provide some monetary redress" to people let down by institutions, and says the government should seek contributions to it from the institutions affected [S-03]. The cited sources give no compensation totals or payment figures [S-01][S-02][S-03].
