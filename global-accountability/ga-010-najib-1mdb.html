Najib Razak and 1MDB: A Prime Minister Behind Bars

Malaysia · a sourced Global Accountability entry built from court records and official findings.

1 Summary

In July 2020 a Malaysian High Court found former Prime Minister Najib Razak guilty of abuse of power, money laundering and criminal breach of trust over funds transferred from SRC International, a former unit of 1MDB, to his personal account [S-02]. On August 23, 2022 the five-member Federal Court unanimously upheld the conviction and sentence, calling it "a simple and straightforward case of abuse of power, criminal breach of trust and money laundering" [S-01][S-02]. He began his sentence that day, the first former Malaysian prime minister to be imprisoned [S-02]. On December 26, 2025 the High Court found him guilty on four counts of abuse of power and 21 counts of money laundering in the main 1MDB trial, and sentenced him to 15 years [S-03][S-04]. The new term takes effect after his SRC term ends on August 23, 2028, and his counsel said the defence would appeal [S-04].

2 Follow the money

  • SRC International: RM42 million was transferred from SRC International into Najib's personal accounts [S-02][S-03]. He was fined RM210 million on top of the 12-year sentence, which the BBC reports was later halved [S-02][S-03].
  • 1MDB main trial: the convictions concern RM2.3 billion in 1MDB-linked funds received through an AmIslamic Bank branch in Kuala Lumpur [S-04]. The court imposed a total fine of RM11.4 billion and ordered him to pay a recoverable sum of RM2,081,476,926 under Malaysia's anti-money-laundering law [S-04].
  • Wider scandal: the BBC reports investigators' estimate that $4.5bn was siphoned from 1MDB [S-03]. That figure is an estimate, not a court finding [S-03].
