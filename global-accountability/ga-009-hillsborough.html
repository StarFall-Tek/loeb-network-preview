Global Accountability GA-009

Hillsborough: Altered Police Statements and an Unlawful-Killing Verdict

United Kingdom (England) · a sourced Global Accountability entry built from court records and official findings.

1 Summary

The Hillsborough Independent Panel found that 116 of the 164 South Yorkshire Police statements identified for substantive amendment "were amended to remove or alter comments unfavourable to SYP" [S-02]. It also found that officers were discouraged from criticising senior officers or describing deficiencies in the police response [S-02]. After the Panel's report, the original accidental-death verdicts were quashed and new inquests were held [S-03]. On April 26, 2016 the inquest jury concluded that the 96 victims of the 1989 disaster were unlawfully killed, that police errors caused a dangerous situation at the turnstiles, and that Liverpool fans did not contribute to the danger [S-03]. No one has been convicted over the alleged cover-up: in May 2021 two retired officers and a former force solicitor were acquitted when the judge ruled they had no case to answer [S-04].

2 Follow the money

The cited official findings document no bribes, payments or fines [S-02][S-03]. In its analysis of the 2019 verdict, the BBC reported that about £65m had been spent on the criminal investigation [S-05]. That figure comes from the BBC report, not from an official finding [S-05].
