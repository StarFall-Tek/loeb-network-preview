Global Accountability GA-003

Australia's Royal Commission: Cultures of Secrecy

1 Summary

Australia's Royal Commission into Institutional Responses to Child Sexual Abuse delivered its Final Report to the Governor-General on December 15, 2017, after a five-year inquiry [S-01][S-02]. The Commission held 57 public hearings and 8,013 private sessions, and the Final Report contains 3,955 de-identified survivor narratives [S-01][S-02]. Its CEO said the public had learned of "the multiple and persistent failings of institutions to keep children safe, the cultures of secrecy and cover-up" [S-01]. The Final Report makes 189 new recommendations, 409 across all of the Commission's reports, including 58 for religious institutions [S-01][S-02].

2 Follow the money

The Australian Government provided the Royal Commission with $372.8 million over five years, and its estimated total spending was $342.3 million [S-01]. Redress and Civil Litigation was the subject of one of the Commission's separate earlier reports [S-01][S-02]. The cited sources give no redress payment totals [S-01][S-02].
